## 7. Assumptions & Blockers

Blockers: none that stop planning.

Assumptions / UNVERIFIED (each to be settled in sandbox or marked `YOUR CALL` at implementation):

1. **Brand = Maxio product family** (`brand-<CatalogBrand.Id>` handle), created on demand; plans are products in those families. Maxio has no other way to attach a brand to a product. The plan list shown to shoppers is the non-archived products in brand families only (other site products are not offered). `YOUR CALL — not in the map`.
2. **Card-less subscribe**: `payment_collection_method` is sent as `remittance` (Relationship Invoicing) — if the sandbox site is on legacy Statements, `invoice` is the card-less value instead; **UNVERIFIED which architecture**. If Maxio answers 422 (card required), the endpoint returns 422 with Maxio's reason; do not fabricate a card. Products are created with `RequireCreditCard = false`.
3. **Shopper identity**: JWT `ClaimTypes.Name` is the only identity claim; it is used as Maxio customer `reference`. Email comes from the Identity user store; first/last name are placeholders (`YOUR CALL`).
4. **Switch on a canceled subscription** uses `UpdateSubscription.ProductHandle` (migration refuses non-active/trialing); acceptance by Maxio for `canceled` is not stated in the remarks — **UNVERIFIED**, test in sandbox; if Maxio refuses, the endpoint returns 4xx with its reason (not a silent reactivate-and-migrate). Switch is immediate on product; the amount changes at the next period (no proration) — migration with proration was not chosen. `YOUR CALL`.
5. **`paid`** = `BalanceInCents == 0`; null balance -> 502. Relationship Invoicing's meaning of `balance_in_cents` vs open invoices is **UNVERIFIED** (the Invoices controller was not planned).
6. **Usage-for-a-day** granularity: the day is the calendar date of `Usage.CreatedAt` (recording time in Maxio), time zone = whatever offset Maxio returns (site zone not exposed by the SDK) — **UNVERIFIED**; only `metered_component` components are summed/listed; event-based (Ebb) usage is not available via this SDK's `ListUsages` and is out of scope.
7. **Currency price unit**: SDK `Price` is `int`; unit (major vs minor) is **UNVERIFIED**; only whole numbers accepted; only `baseline` role sent.
8. **Next billing date**: sent as a `DateTimeOffset` at a fixed time of day (`YOUR CALL`); Maxio silently normalises invalid dates and does not echo `next_billing_at`, so the handler verifies via `CurrentPeriodEndsAt`. Calendar-billing (`snap_day`) subscriptions are rejected with 422.
9. **Restart** = `ReactivateSubscription` with no body (new billing period, may charge); `Resume` flag not used. `YOUR CALL`.
10. **Claims**: the app-owned `MaxioClaim` table needs an EF migration on SQL Server; in-memory test DB enforces the PK. A duplicate request after the first finished is prevented by Maxio-side state checks (existing non-canceled subscription to the same plan handle for that customer => 409), not by a retained claim row.
11. 4xx mapping rule for every endpoint: Maxio 404 on a shopper's subscription = our 404; Maxio 422/400 = our 422 with Maxio's message(s); Maxio 401/403 (our credentials) and unreachable/timeouts = 502/504 with a generic reason (not 4xx — the shopper cannot fix it); an SDK exception never reaches `ExceptionMiddleware`.

## 8. Settled at implementation (YOUR CALL decisions)

- **Claims are process-local** (`MaxioBillingService.Claim`, a static `ConcurrentDictionary`), not a DB table: no EF migration was added. A repeat after the first call finishes is not blocked (one shopper may hold the same plan twice; the task does not forbid it). Cross-instance races are not prevented.
- **Brand = product family** `brand-<CatalogBrand.Id>`, created on demand by `EnsureFamily`; the plan list is the non-archived, no-card products in those families (`OfferedPlans`).
- **Customer names** are placeholders (`<username local part>` / `Shopper`); email from the Identity store (`EnsureCustomer`).
- **`paid`** = `BalanceInCents <= 0` (zero or in credit means nothing owed); null balance -> 502 (`ToDto`, `GetSubscription`).
- **Switch** uses `UpdateSubscription.ProductHandle` for every state; verified in the sandbox on a cancelled subscription (stays cancelled on the new plan until restarted); product re-read and checked (`Switch`).
- **Restart** = `ReactivateSubscription` without body (new billing period).
- **Next billing date** sent at 12:00 UTC of the requested date, verified from `CurrentPeriodEndsAt` within one day (`SetNextBillingDate`).
- **Currency prices**: whole numbers only; the sandbox site defines only its base currency, so other currencies return Maxio's 422 reason (surfaced as 422).
- **Card-less subscribe** sends `payment_collection_method = remittance` (works on the sandbox site).
- **Usage day** = calendar date of `CreatedAt` as Maxio returns it.
